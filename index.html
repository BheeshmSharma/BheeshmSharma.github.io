PhD Research Sciences Intern

Microsoft Research, Hyderabad, India
Summer 2026

Worked as a PhD Research Sciences Intern on developing an Automatic Prompt Optimization (APO) framework for optimizing large system prompts used across AI-powered product features. The project focused on automatically refining manually engineered system prompts to improve response quality while reducing prompt length and inference cost.

  • Developed an Automatic Prompt Optimization (APO) pipeline for optimizing production-scale system prompts.
  • Improved prompt quality while significantly reducing token usage, lowering inference cost and latency.
  • Designed optimization strategies that preserve model behavior while producing more concise and effective prompts.
  • Contributed toward scalable prompt engineering techniques for Large Language Model (LLM) based product features.

About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (IEOR) at IIT Bombay, advised by Prof. Balamurugan Palaniappan. My research centers on efficient deep learning and multimodal generative AI for medical imaging, as well as real-world image and video generation.

I specialize in few-shot, weakly supervised, and self-supervised learning paradigms — building AI systems that learn efficiently from limited data while holding up in critical, high-stakes applications.

Medical Imaging Vision-Language Models Multimodal Generative AI Zero/Few-Shot Learning Automatic Prompt Optimization

Working with

  • Diffusion & denoising generative models
  • Weakly-supervised anomaly detection
  • Brain MRI & multi-modal medical scans
  • Vision-language & sign-to-audio translation
  • Large-scale system prompt optimization
